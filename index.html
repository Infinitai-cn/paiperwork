01 · Paiperwork

Paperwork, handled.

Paiperwork is a privacy-first AI document & productivity tool powered by Ollama. Chat with your documents, translate and visualize data, research the web, draft from templates, and create presentations, artifacts and campaigns — all on-device, encrypted with your Master Key, with zero data leaving your machine.

  • Chat & document intelligence

    Talk to your PDFs and text files — ask questions, get comprehensive summaries, and search your whole collection by meaning, not just keywords.

  • Research & knowledge

    AI-powered web research with deep search, organized reports with sources, and a personal semantic knowledge base.

  • Translate, visualize & create

    Translate documents, turn data into charts from plain language, use professional templates, and build presentations, artifacts and campaigns.

  • Private by design

    Runs locally on Ollama, encrypted with your Master Key, with adjustable context. No telemetry, no data collection.

02 · Ania3D

Compose worlds on your terms.

Ania3D turns plain language into 3D. Describe a scene and your local LLM builds it — geometry, materials, lighting, camera. Render it photorealistic with a click, or generate images and video right inside the viewport.

  • Describe it, get a scene

    Tell the sidebar what you picture — “a lighthouse on a stormy coast” — and a local LLM builds it: geometry, materials, lighting, camera. Keep chatting to refine; only your changes are applied, live.

  • Photorealistic, in real time

    A 60 fps WebGL viewport with one-click GPU path tracing, soft shadows, HDRI and procedural environments — plus living weather: rain with puddles, snow, animated water, volumetric fire and smoke.

  • Bring your own keys

    Connect the AI providers you already use — Kling, MiniMax, ComfyUI, Ollama, Sketchfab, ShareTextures. You pay them directly. No markup, no lock-in, ever.

  • AI media, in-scene

    Generate images and video from text or straight from your viewport — text-to-image, image-to-image, text-to-video, image-to-video — then drop the results into the scene as backdrops, sprites or playing video.
