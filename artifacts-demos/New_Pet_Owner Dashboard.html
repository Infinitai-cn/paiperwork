First Vet Visit

Schedule within 7-10 days
▼
  • Schedule appointment within first week of adoption
  • Bring adoption records and any previous medical history
  • Bring stool sample for parasite screening (within 24 hours)
  • Discuss microchipping with your veterinarian
  • Ask about spay/neuter timeline and recommendations
⚠️
Important: Keep your cat in a secure carrier during the car ride to reduce stress.

Nutrition & Feeding

Age-appropriate diet guide
▼
  • Feed high-quality commercial cat food (AAFCO approved)
  • Kittens: 3-4 meals daily until 6 months
  • Adults: 2 meals daily after 6 months
  • Always provide fresh, clean water
  • Avoid human foods (especially toxic: chocolate, onions, garlic)
✨

Grooming & Hygiene

Daily and weekly care routine
▼
  • Scoop litter box daily, full change weekly
  • Brush 1-2 times weekly (daily for long-haired breeds)
  • Trim nails every 2-3 weeks
  • Check and clean ears monthly
  • Dental check-ups recommended annually
🚨

Warning Signs to Watch

Know when to call the vet
▼
🤮 Vomiting
💩 Diarrhea
🚰 Excessive thirst
🍽️ Loss of appetite
😴 Lethargy
🤧 Sneezing/coughing
🚨
Emergency: Difficulty breathing, seizures, inability to urinate, or collapse require immediate veterinary care.
🛒

Essential Supplies

What you need to get started
▼
  • Litter box + litter (one per cat + one extra)
  • Quality cat food (wet and dry)
  • Food and water bowls (stainless steel or ceramic)
  • Scratching post or cat tree
  • Comfortable bed and hiding spots
  • Cat carrier for vet visits

First Vet Visit

Schedule within 48-72 hours
▼
  • Schedule appointment within 48-72 hours of bringing puppy home
  • Bring breeder/rescue medical records and vaccination history
  • Bring fresh stool sample for intestinal parasite test
  • Discuss microchipping - recommended for all dogs
  • Ask about flea, tick, and heartworm prevention schedule
✅
Tip: Bring treats to create positive associations with vet visits early on.
💉

Nutrition & Feeding

Puppy vs Adult feeding guide
▼
  • Choose AAFCO-approved puppy food for dogs under 12 months
  • Puppies: Feed 3-4 times daily until 4 months
  • Reduce to 2 meals daily after 6 months
  • Measure portions based on weight and activity level
  • Never feed: chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, xylitol
💡
Growth Monitoring: Large breed puppies should eat specific large breed puppy food to prevent rapid bone growth.
🎾

Training & Exercise

Physical and mental development
▼
  • Begin house training at 8-12 weeks old
  • Start basic commands (sit, stay, come) by 12 weeks
  • Socialize during critical period (8-16 weeks)
  • Exercise appropriate for age (5 min × months old for puppies)
  • Provide mental stimulation with puzzle toys
⚠️
Caution: Avoid strenuous exercise before 18 months in large breeds to protect developing joints.
🚨

Warning Signs to Watch

Emergency symptoms list
▼
🤮 Vomiting
💩 Bloody stool
🫁 Difficulty breathing
💥 Bloating/swollen abdomen
🦴 Unable to stand
😵 Seizures
🚨
Bloat Emergency: If your dog has a distended stomach, tries to vomit but can't, seek immediate emergency care - this is life-threatening.
🛒

Essential Supplies

New puppy checklist
▼
  • Appropriately sized collar with ID tag
  • 6-foot nylon leash and treats for training
  • Quality puppy food (check with breeder/rescue)
  • Food and water bowls (stable, non-tip design)
  • Crate for sleeping and safe space
  • Chew toys and puzzle feeders
