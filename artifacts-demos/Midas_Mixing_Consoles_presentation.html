The Birth of an Audio Legacy

1970 - The Foundation

Midas was founded in London by visionaries Jeff Byers and Charles Brooke, establishing what would become one of the most respected names in professional audio console manufacturing. Their commitment to uncompromising audio quality set the standard for the industry.

The London Legacy

Operating from the heart of London's thriving music scene, Midas quickly gained recognition among audio engineers and sound professionals who demanded equipment capable of delivering exceptional performance in demanding live environments.

Music Tribe Era

Today, Midas operates as part of the Music Tribe group of brands, continuing the tradition of excellence while embracing modern technological innovations that have expanded the possibilities for sound professionals worldwide.

Professional mixing consoles have been Midas's core expertise for over five decades

Professional Applications

Midas consoles are trusted by audio engineers worldwide for live sound mixing in the most demanding environments. From intimate venues to massive stadium concerts, these consoles deliver the reliability and sonic excellence that professionals demand when every performance matters.

Front of House (FOH) Mixing

The primary position for live sound reinforcement, where engineers control what the audience hears. Midas FOH consoles provide comprehensive control over every aspect of the mix, from channel EQ and dynamics to complex effects processing and spatial imaging.

  • Complete mix control for audience experience
  • Advanced EQ and dynamics processing
  • Real-time adjustments during performances

World's First "Tri-Purpose" Console

The Legend 3000 introduced the revolutionary concept of a console that could seamlessly handle Front of House, monitor mixing, and recording applications all from a single platform, fundamentally changing how venues and engineers approached system design.

Modular Flat PCB Design

The innovative modular design improved reliability and serviceability, allowing engineers to easily swap modules and maintain consistent performance across demanding touring schedules without extended downtime.

The Digital Revolution: PRO6

In September 2008 at the annual PLASA tradeshow, Midas introduced the PRO6 Live Audio System, marking a significant milestone as their second networked digital audio system. This launch represented the culmination of technologies developed from years of research and real-world feedback from professional engineers.

Networked Digital Architecture

The PRO6 brought networked digital audio distribution to the forefront, allowing engineers to manage complex signal routing with unprecedented flexibility while maintaining the legendary Midas audio quality that professionals expected.

Key Innovations

  • Advanced digital snake connectivity
  • Comprehensive network integration
  • Real-time system monitoring
  • Seamless analog-to-digital workflow
Digital consoles brought unprecedented control to live sound environments

Digital Audio Distribution Systems

Beyond mixing consoles, Midas markets comprehensive digital audio distribution components that serve as the backbone of modern live sound installations. These systems are commonly used with their digital consoles as stand-alone digital snakes, providing the connectivity infrastructure that complex productions demand.

Digital snake systems enable flexible signal routing across venues

Stand-Alone Digital Snake Capability

Midas digital audio distribution components can function independently as sophisticated digital snake systems, allowing venues to route audio signals across vast distances with pristine quality and minimal latency, transforming how productions are designed and executed.

System Advantages

  • Scalable Architecture: Systems grow with production requirements

Engineering Excellence: Key Features

Every Midas console incorporates decades of engineering expertise, delivering features that professional audio engineers rely upon for critical applications in live performance and studio environments.

Superior Preamp Design

Midas microphone preamplifiers are renowned throughout the industry for their pristine audio quality, exceptional headroom, and the warm, natural sound character that engineers describe as the "Midas Blue" signature. These preamps faithfully capture every nuance of the source material.

Comprehensive Dynamics

Each channel features professional-grade dynamics processing including compression, limiting, and gating. The musical nature of Midas dynamics allows for transparent control that enhances rather than colors the audio, maintaining the integrity of the original performance.

Precise Equalization

The legendary Midas EQ sections provide surgical precision for shaping sound. With carefully selected frequency bands and Q characteristics, engineers can make subtle tonal corrections or dramatic creative decisions with equal confidence and control.

Robust Build Quality

Designed for the demands of touring and permanent installation, Midas consoles feature industrial-grade components, sealed faders, and protective circuitry that ensure reliable operation night after night, venue after venue, in every conceivable environment.
